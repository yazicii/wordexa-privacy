# Wordexa Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Wordexa** (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Data controller

**Wordexa**  
Contact: [privacy@wordexa.app](mailto:privacy@wordexa.app)

## 2. Summary

- **No account** is required to play Wordexa.
- Your game progress (XP, coins, missions, settings, etc.) is stored **locally on your device**.
- The App may show ads through **Google AdMob**.
- In-game events (e.g. level up, hint used) may be logged for analytics; **word text is not sent** to our servers.
- We do **not sell** your data.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Data stored on your device (local storage)

The App stores the following on your device (via `SharedPreferences`) to provide the game experience:

| Data type | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| XP, level, coin balance | Progression and economy |
| Run / puzzle state | Resume unfinished games |
| Statistics (words found, longest word length, combos, etc.) | Stats screen |
| Achievements and daily mission progress | Game mechanics |
| Sound and vibration preferences | Settings |
| Daily streak information | Streak rewards |
| Onboarding completion status | First-launch flow |
| Recently used main words (anti-repeat) | Game balance |

This data stays **on your device** and is not automatically uploaded to Wordexa servers.

Removing the App from your device or using in-app reset options (where available) deletes local data.

### 3.2 Analytics data

We may record **anonymous or pseudonymous** events such as:

- App open, run start / end
- Word submission (valid/invalid, **length only**, and combo — **not the word itself**)
- Hint and power-up usage
- Level up, achievement, and task events

Today these events may be written to device logs in development. We may enable services such as Firebase Analytics in the future; this policy will be updated accordingly.

### 3.3 Advertising data

The App uses **Google AdMob** (banner and rewarded ads). Google may process data such as advertising ID, device information, and ad interactions under its own privacy policy.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy  
- You can limit personalized ads in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy → Advertising; Android: Google settings → Ads).

### 3.4 What we do not collect

Wordexa does **not** collect:

- Name, email, or phone number (no account system)
- Precise location
- Contacts or photo library access
- Microphone or camera recordings

Dictionary files are bundled with the App; words you type are not sent to a server.

## 4. How we use information

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---------|-------------------|
| Operating the game and saving progress | Contract performance |
| Showing ads | Legitimate interests / consent (platform rules) |
| Debugging and product improvement | Legitimate interests |
| Legal compliance | Legal obligation |

## 5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your data. Limited sharing may occur with:

| Provider | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Google AdMob | Ad delivery |
| Google (future analytics) | Usage statistics |

These providers are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 6. Children’s privacy

Wordexa is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact [privacy@wordexa.app](mailto:privacy@wordexa.app) and we will delete it.

## 7. Retention

- **Local game data:** Until you delete the App or reset data on device.
- **Ad / analytics data:** Subject to the relevant third party’s retention policies.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under **GDPR**, **UK GDPR**, **CCPA**, or **Turkish KVKK**, including:

- Access, correction, deletion
- Restriction or objection to processing
- Data portability (where applicable)
- Withdraw consent (where processing is consent-based)

Because most data is stored only on your device, deletion is often achieved by uninstalling the App. For other requests, email [privacy@wordexa.app](mailto:privacy@wordexa.app); we aim to respond within 30 days.

## 9. International transfers

Ad and analytics providers may process data in the United States and other countries. See Google’s policies for safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change; material updates may be communicated in the App.
